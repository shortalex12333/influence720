# Dashboard 5: Consolidated Micro Tactical Warfare (SFDC × Epic Single Account)

> **Document Status**: Definitive Specification & Architecture Guide for Dashboard 5 (D5).
> **Scope**: Micro / Single Account Consolidation of **Salesforce CRM (D1)** + **Applied Epic ERP (D2)**.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: What D5 IS and IS NOT

### What D5 IS:
* **The Micro Integration of D1 + D2**: D5 takes the **Named Contact & Referral Network from Salesforce (D1)** and merges it directly with the **Priced Forensic Whitespace & Coverage Gaps from Epic (D2)** for a single client account.
* **The Producer Persona Pitch**:
  > *"My client is X (Epic Paying Account), he knows contact ABC (Salesforce), who works with structural role DDD (360 Ontology). Client X can introduce me next time I see him because role DDD needs XYZ policies (Coverage Corpus Data Layer)."*
* **The "Trojan Horse" Execution Blueprint**: It answers the ultimate producer question: *"Because I know Person A in Salesforce, how do I capture Missing Policy B in Epic?"*
* **Single-Account Focus**: Applied to one client ecosystem at a time, avoiding multi-client spiderwebs.

### What D5 IS NOT:
* ❌ **NOT a Macro Leadership Map**: D5 does not aggregate regional books or rep scatters. (That is Dashboard 6).
* ❌ **NOT Speculative AI Matching**: It uses hard deterministic graph edges (`referred_from` in SFDC → `responsible_role` in 360 → `unplaced_policy` in Epic).
