## 2. Inviolable Architectural Principles & Unified UX Pattern

1. **The Invariant Radial Wheel Canvas**: The visual form NEVER changes from the Radial Wheel Canvas Knowledge Graph. No flat tables, no card grids, no pie charts, no mini-donuts.
2. **Unified Movable, Scrollable, Zoomable, Clickable UX Engine**:
   * **Movable & Scrollable**: Smooth click-and-drag pan across the canvas space.
   * **Zoomable**: Smooth mouse-wheel zoom centered on cursor position + zoom control (+/-) buttons.
   * **Clickable**: Every node (Structural Role, Contact, Entity, Policy Line) is directly interactive.
   * **Expand & Cascade upon Click**: Clicking a node **EXPANDS & CASCADES** the underlying hierarchy of information directly onto the radial wheel canvas (revealing edges, policy satellites, warm intro paths, and statutory breach lines).
3. **Zero AI / Zero Hallucinations**: All logic remains deterministic expert-system rules, hard database lookups, and exact math.
4. **Total Stillness by Default**: No `@keyframes` animations, auto-scrolling tickers, or moving nodes.
5. **Strict System Separation**: CRM intimacy vs. ERP policy ledgers remain separate until consolidated views (D5 & D6).
