## 1. Executive Summary: What D2 IS and IS NOT

### What D2 IS:
* **A Single-Account Forensic Audit**: A diagnostic mask applied strictly to **Applied Epic ERP** data for one client ecosystem at a time (navigated via a Left Rail queue).
* **The "What COULD Happen Tomorrow" Engine**: It contrasts what is bound in Epic (*Yesterday*) against what the asset cascade structurally mandates (*Tomorrow*), exposing unhedged legal liabilities, limit deficiencies, and uncaptured GWP.
* **Driven by Deterministic Rules & Coverage Corpus DB**: Zero AI, zero predictive ML, zero hallucinations. Every gap is derived from hard asset parameters, statutory laws, and database limit comparisons.
* **Structural Role Responsibility**: Nodes represent **Structural Roles & Legal Entities** (e.g., SPV Director, Trustee, Yacht Captain, General Counsel), isolating who holds legal/operational liability for missing coverages.

### What D2 IS NOT:
* ❌ **NOT a Salesforce CRM Tool**: D2 does not track personal contact intimacy, phone calls, or warm referral paths. (That is strictly **Dashboard 1**).
* ❌ **NOT a Multi-Client Spiderweb**: D2 never dumps an entire book or multiple clients onto one canvas.
* ❌ **NOT a Generic PDF Coverage Checklist**: D2 does not present flat lists of insurance products (*Auto, Umbrella*). It presents a dynamic **Asset-to-Human Wealth Cascade**.
* ❌ **NOT a Speculative Sales Bot**: D2 does not issue cheesy CRM "actionable prompts" or guess premium figures out of thin air.

### 2.2 Filtration Metrics (D2 Filter Controls)
1. **Left Rail Target Queue Sorting**: Filter/sort accounts by *Lowest Penetration %*, *Highest Whitespace GWP ($)*, or *Expiring Retention Risk (<90d)*.
2. **Persona & Scale Simulator**: Slider to switch from *Known Epic Asset Mode* (e.g. 24m Yacht) → *Family Office Upgrade* (simulates 70m Superyacht cascade / $100M+ portfolio).
3. **Bound Status Filter**: Filter policy satellites by status (Bound, Expiring <90d, Unplaced Whitespace Gap).
4. **Fiduciary Severity Filter**: Filter gaps by legal exposure (Critical Signatory exposed, High Asset exposed, Operational gap).

## 3. Micro UX & Interaction Flow

1. **Select Account from Left Rail Queue**: Ranked by *Lowest Penetration / Highest Whitespace Ratio*.
2. **Inspect 360 Radial Canvas**: Center displays Named Insured; canvas displays active Asset Verticals. Green satellites show bound Epic policies; **greyed-out satellites** show unplaced whitespace brought forward from the Coverage Corpus DB.
3. **Click a Greyed-Out Whitespace Badge**: The Right Drawer slides out displaying:
   * **Coverage Line Name**: e.g., *Helideck Third-Party Liability*.
   * **Status**: *Unplaced in Epic (Competitor Whitespace or Uninsured)*.
   * **Required Baseline Limit**: *$10,000,000*.
   * **Responsible Structural Role**: *SPV Director (Ring 1)* & *Yacht Captain (Ring 1)*.
   * **Fiduciary Risk Note**: Explains the exact legal trapdoor.
   * **Defensible GWP Benchmark**: *$18,500/yr* (Calculated via Peer Comp against peer 70m yachts in Epic).
4. **Toggle Persona / Scale Simulator**: Slide to simulate unlisted assets (*24m Yacht → 70m Superyacht Cascade*), dynamically expanding the 360 whitespace map.
