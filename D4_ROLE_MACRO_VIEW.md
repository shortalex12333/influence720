# Dashboard 4: Role & Coverage Macro View (Leadership Book Rollup)

> **Document Status**: Definitive Specification & Architecture Guide for Dashboard 4 (D4).
> **Scope**: Applied Epic ERP (Macro / Book of Record) × Coverage Corpus Data Layer × 360 Relationship Ontology.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: What D4 IS and IS NOT

### What D4 IS:
* **The Macro Aggregation of D2**: While D2 audits a single client account, **D4 aggregates the entire regional book of record in Applied Epic** for Sales Leadership, Department Heads, and Managing Directors.
* **The Portfolio Whitespace & Domain Heatmap**: It compares total bound premium in Epic against total structural market capacity (the sum of all Coverage Corpus requirements across all regional assets).
* **Strategic Campaign & Resource Allocation Engine**: It gives leadership clear empirical evidence to decide whether to launch **Cross-Sell Conversion Campaigns** on existing accounts OR **New Domain Expansion Initiatives** (recruiting specialized brokers for uncaptured risk sectors).

### What D4 IS NOT:
* ❌ **NOT a CRM Activity Tracker**: D4 does not track phone calls or emails. (That is D3/D5).
* ❌ **NOT a Single-Account View**: D4 operates exclusively at the macro portfolio level.
* ❌ **NOT Speculative Market Modeling**: D4 only measures whitespace generated by *actual accounts currently on our book in Epic*, combined with Coverage Corpus DB rules.

## 3. Visual Layout & Geometry on D4

### 3.1 Macro Radial Coordinate System

D4 reuses the 360 radial structure at the **Domain & Coverage Line Level**:

* **9 Radial Sectors (The 9 Operational Lifecycle Domains)**: Aggregates total bound vs. missing premium across all 9 domains (*Ownership & Structure, Legal & Finance, Compliance, Insurance Market, Acquisition, Build/Refit, Operations & Staff, Usage & Experience, Shoreside*).
* **Concentric Rings (Policy Line Hierarchy)**:
  * *Inner Ring (Bound Premium)*: Total annual GWP currently bound and indemnified in Epic.
  * *Outer Ring (Uncaptured Whitespace)*: Total unplaced GWP brought forward from the Coverage Corpus Data Layer across all active regional assets.

### 3.2 Producer Gap Skew & Department Performance

* **Producer Breakdown View**: Leadership can toggle from *Domain View* to *Producer View* to inspect which sales reps are successfully capturing multi-line cascades vs. which reps are leaving millions in greyed-out whitespace on the table.
* **Carrier Concentration Radar**: Highlights carrier risk (e.g. 75% of regional Hull bound with a single syndicate), allowing leadership to negotiate better commission terms or redistribute market placement.

## 4. Summary Matrix: Micro (D2) vs. Macro (D4)

| Dimension | Dashboard 2 (Micro View) | Dashboard 4 (Macro View) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary User** | Individual Producer / Account Executive | Sales Leadership / Managing Director |
| **Scope** | Single Client Ecosystem (1 Account) | Entire Regional Book of Record (Plural Accounts) |
| **Primary Question** | *"What coverages is THIS client missing?"* | *"Which domains/lines are we under-penetrated in across the firm?"* |
| **Action Driven** | Fiduciary Account Audit & Client Briefing | Cross-Sell Campaigns & Domain Recruitment Strategy |
| **Data Sourced** | Single Epic Account + Coverage Corpus DB | Aggregated Epic Ledger + Coverage Corpus DB |
