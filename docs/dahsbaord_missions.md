Dashboard 2 — actual sales imprivemtn for AOV/GWP per client (MICRO) Engine
The WEIGHT and VALUE of that same book in dollars — for each account, which specific coverage lines on assets we already service are unbound (priced, actionable), plus which entire assets might exist that we have zero record of at all (undiscovered exposure).

Dashboard 3 — Leadership Book Rollup (MACRO)
Every producer's book at once, from a manager's seat — who on the team holds how much value, which of their accounts are healthy/expiring/lost, where regional undiscovered exposure concentrates.
→ Subject: producers, plural. Scope: the whole team. Question: "whose book needs my attention, and where?"
Same underlying whitespace concept as D2, but the unit changes from "one account" to "one producer" — this is D2 rolled up, not a new idea.

Dashboard 4 — epic Macro View
Across the whole region and all 5 verticals, which specific roles — not accounts, not producers — are well-penetrated vs. barely touched region-wide, and which salesman already has the standing relationship to attack an underexploited one.

Dashboard 5 — Executive Command Center (salesforce + epic)
Producer velocity and risk over time — who's converting touchpoints into GWP efficiently vs. grinding for nothing, whose book is silently decaying before a renewal, where dollar-whitespace concentrates by policy line region-wide.
→ Subject: trend and decay. Scope: the whole region, over a time window. Question: "who's winning, who's about to lose an account, and where's the market moving?"
The only one of the five that's about change, not a snapshot — every other dashboard answers "what's true right now," this one answers "what's true right now compared to 90 days ago."

The one-sentence differentiator, if you need to defend all 5 in a room: D1=who, D2=what's it worth, D3=whose book (rolled up), D4=which function (sliced sideways), D5=which way is it moving. Same underlying dataset, five genuinely different questions — none of them answerable by looking at any of the others.
